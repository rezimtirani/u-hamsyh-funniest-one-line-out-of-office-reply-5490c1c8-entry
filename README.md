One single-line out-of-office email auto-reply: the sender is away until Monday, and the inbox has been handed to Dave — who only found out about it when this reply reached him.

> Out of office until Monday. Your email is being handled by Dave, who only found out about it when he got this reply.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the reply to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 160 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
